How to read this table
Row color = stock runway
Red — out of stock, no order placed
Amber — out of stock, shipment on the way
Orange — stocks out within 60 days
Yellow — stocks out within 6 months
Green — safe for 6+ months
Column guide
Units to BuyHow many to put on the next PO — at minimum enough to cover one full lead time of demand Purchase CostTotal cost of that PO (units × cost/unit). Click the input to enter cost if missing. VelocityAverage daily Shopify sales since this product first sold Lead TimeEditable — months from placing a PO to receiving stock. Drives all date calculations. Low Risk ReorderOrder by this date to have a comfortable buffer before running out Exact Reorder DateLatest date to place the order — stock arrives just as you sell the last unit IncomingCheck this when a PO is placed — row turns amber instead of red
